AfriHealth AI Product Strategy

Purpose and product direction

AfriHealth AI is a clinician-reviewed voice documentation and decision-support workflow for community health teams working across English, Amharic-English, and Afaan Oromoo-English conversations.

1. Product modules

Module 1: Triage

Captures reviewed audio, returns code-switched transcription, and presents a clinician-review triage summary.

Module 2: EHR Intake

Structures reviewed speech into draft documentation and coding artifacts. Vital signs require manual confirmation.

Module 3: Post-Care

Supports follow-up simulations and highlights possible escalation signals for human assessment.

2. Evidence and governance

Fixture benchmark values, AfriSwitch general-ASR results, and the 15-case clinical-review baseline are shown as separate evidence layers. The measured clinical baseline is 56.38% mean WER, 44.33% target-term recall, and six critical-term-miss cases. These results require clinician review and do not support autonomous care.

3. Privacy and safety

Provider credentials remain server-side. Recordings are reviewed before upload, private validation inputs remain outside Git, and medication suggestions are blocked until clinician confirmation and patient context are supplied.
